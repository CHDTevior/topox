## §1 — What M1.7 is

The project pivoted from the M1.5R fk6 line to **integrating AnyTop's
pre-processed Truebones dataset** and aligning the Graph-VAE with AnyTop's
native motion representation.

- M1.5R 4-way P3 training was launched 2026-05-21 then the user pivoted: instead
  of fixing our own preprocessed data, use AnyTop's already-clean processed
  dataset. All M1.5R training pythons were killed (allocs retained).
- Data — 1070 motions / 70 object types / 13-channel RIFKE encoding
  (0:3 RIFKE/relative pos | 3:9 6D rot | 9:12 velocity | 12 foot contact).
  A LOCAL COPY (motions/ + cond.npy + motion_texts_by_file.json, ~453M) now
  lives at `data/anytop_truebones/` and is the dataset default
  (`_DEFAULT_ANYTOP_ROOT`) — decouples training from the external AnyTop repo.
  AnyTop source (read-only, never modified):
  `/iridisfs/scratch/ts1v23/workspace/Anytop/AnyTop/dataset/truebones/zoo/truebones_processed/`.
