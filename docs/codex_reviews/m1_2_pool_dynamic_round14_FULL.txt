New 22nd R12

Finding: sub-1e-6 parent/adjacency mismatch is accepted, but Floyd treats it as a true edge.

Why it is R12:
- The parent-vs-adjacency consistency check is the Round 14 fix for #21, but uses allclose with atol=1e-6 at src/models/graph_salad/pool_dynamic.py:702.
- graph_utils.floyd_shortest_path defines has_edge = (adjacency > 0) at src/models/graph_salad/graph_utils.py:128. Therefore adjacency[0, 0, 5] = 5e-7 is topologically a real edge for Floyd, but "close enough to zero" for the parent consistency check.
- If geodesic_dist is recomputed from that adjacency, Floyd consistency passes too, because expected_geo is based on the corrupted adjacency at src/models/graph_salad/pool_dynamic.py:623-640.
- Actual behavior is NO_RAISE with a shortcut distance of 1 hop between joints 0 and 5, although parent_indices describes a line where 0->5 should be 5 hops.

Required fixes before GO

   Either reject non-matching device with ValueError or normalize anchor_indices/coarse_mask to joint_features.device immediately after shape/dtype checks and before range/active-slot validation. Add meta or mixed-device regression.

   Use torch.equal on masked finite entries, or allclose with atol=0, rtol=0. Add a sub-1e-6 perturbation regression and a finite-pattern mismatch regression.
